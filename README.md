## Project Overview

This project explores Washington State's registered electric vehicle population using Power BI.


The analysis examines the competitive landscape, geographic distribution of registered EVs and changes in the EV population over time. The goal is to identify the manufacturers and models with the largest presence, understand where registrations are concentrated and examine the changing balance between fully electric vehicles and plug-in hybrids.

## Business Questions

The project focuses on three questions:

1. **Competition:** Which manufacturers and models dominate Washington's registered EV market, and how concentrated is the market?
2. **Geography:** How are EV registrations distributed across counties, cities and electric utility service territories?
3. **Market Evolution:** How has the registered EV population changed over time, and how has the balance between BEVs and PHEVs evolved?

## Dashboard

The dashboard is organised into three sections:

### 1. Competitive Landscape
Examines manufacturer market shares, leading EV models and the concentration of registrations among the largest manufacturers.

### 2. Geographic Distribution
Explores how registrations are distributed across Washington's counties and cities, as well as the recorded electric utility service territories.

### 3. Market Evolution
Examines the historical EV population, annual growth and the changing composition of BEVs and PHEVs.

## Key Findings

- Tesla accounts for approximately **40.71%** of recorded EVs.
- The top five manufacturers account for **63.08%** of the registered population.
- The Tesla Model Y is the most represented model in the dataset, with approximately 66,000 records.
- King County has the largest recorded EV population, with more than 144,000 vehicles.
- The registered EV population reached approximately 302,000 in 2025.
- BEVs account for approximately 80.59% of the latest recorded historical population.

## Scope and Limitations

The vehicle-level analysis is filtered to Washington State registrations. The historical dataset is used to examine changes in the recorded EV population over time.

Historical population figures are snapshots, not monthly sales or new registrations. The 2026 figure is incomplete and is not treated as a final annual total.

The analysis describes registered vehicles. It does not directly measure consumer demand, sales, profitability or charging infrastructure availability.
